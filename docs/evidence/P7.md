# P7 — the top of the funnel: noun-phrase mining and measured output quality

*Not a phase from either dossier. This is the answer to "is the top-10 actually product gaps?", and
the work that question required. Every number below comes from a live run on real sources; the
commands are re-runnable.*

## The question, and the answer

**Question (the human's, after the first live nights):** run it for real and say honestly whether
anything in the results is a real product gap.

**Answer after the first night: no.** The machinery worked end to end — collection, mining, scoring,
cited gated judgement, all live, all at $0 — but the output was mostly sentence fragments:

```
'filament thanks'      Conversational closing remark, not a product gap.
'mouse but i m'        Fragmented text, pure noise.
'bolt which vendors'   Nonsensical, lacks coherent intent.
'time to pick'         Garbled phrase from a gardening query, miscategorized under music audio.
```

The Judge's own reasons. It dropped 15 of 18 candidates, and **67% of those drops were
fragments/noise** — after mining, scoring, ranking and a provider call. Of 25 phrases in the ranked
table, 2 were real product nouns. The single genuine find was **"chisel storage"**, mined from a
woodworker's complaint about cutting foam.

## The diagnosis was measurable, not a hunch

| Symptom | Measurement |
|---|---|
| Fragments reaching the gates | 67% of Judge drops were "fragment / noise / miscategorized" |
| The taxonomy barely engaged with the lake | 17,634 of 17,946 n-grams unmatched (1%) |
| Mis-categorization | "pick and give", "time to pick" filed under `music_audio` (a guitar *pick*), from gardening posts |
| Brand/IP in the candidate pool | `mickey mouse` scored 60.8; only the Judge noticed |
| The lake is small | 708 minable texts, 304 matched phrases, **5** phrases appearing in ≥2 documents |

The system was mostly discovering its own word list. The gates were never the problem: the Judge's
verdicts matched an independent human read of the same list on every candidate.

## What changed

**1. The head-noun rule** (`config/categories.py`). A phrase's category now comes from its **head**
(the last content word), which must be a buyable noun:

* the head is a category's own **core** noun → that category, outright;
* the head is a shared **product-form** noun (`storage`, `holder`, `mount`, `blade`, …) → the category
  comes from the *modifiers*: "chisel storage" → tools_diy;
* a function word immediately before the head is a rejection — that is what "time to pick" is;
* anything else is uncategorized, and the caller drops it.

The `product_nouns` list is why the rule is not a wall: **17 of the form nouns the rule needs were in
no core list** (including `storage`, the only real find of the first night). A head-noun rule without
that vocabulary would have deleted it.

**2. The brand blocklist** (33 entries) — `mickey mouse`, `disney`, `bambu`, `dewalt`, … A branded
product is not a gap, and the first live run mined `bambulabs filament` and `bana filament`.

**3. A language gate** (`_is_foreign`) — a document with ≥2 marker words from Turkish/German/
Spanish/French/Italian/Portuguese/Swedish/Dutch is skipped. Two of ten candidate slots went to
`filament nerisi` and `filament nermeniz` (Turkish for "filament recommendation"); the Judge dropped
both, correctly, after they had been scored. Named failure mode: a short foreign post with no marker
words in it still gets through. The gate skips 6 of 3,753 documents (0.2%).

**4. A diagnosis in the ledger line.** Rejections are counted *by reason*:

```
L1: 763 texts -> 18,807 n-grams -> 53 candidates
    (rejected 18,689: head_not_buyable 11,498, function_word_before_head 6,102,
     numeric_leading 753, brand 207, stale 0, collapsed 57), pruned 43, kept 10
    (5 from a single document)
```

"unmatched 17,634" could not distinguish a small taxonomy from fragmentary mining. This line can.

**5. The apostrophe fix** (`normalize_phrase`). `"I've"` used to normalize to `i` + `ve`, and `"don't"`
to `don` + `t`: junk tokens that leaked into phrases, into the stop-word logic, and — as `ve` is also
Turkish — into the language gate as a false positive on an English espresso post. Apostrophes are now
deleted rather than spaced (`dont`, `ive`).

## Measured outcome

Same sources, same lake, same gates; only the mining and taxonomy changed.

| Metric | Before P7 | After P7 |
|---|---|---|
| Candidates that are product nouns | ~3 of 18 | **10 of 10** in the newest run |
| Judge keep rate (per-run) | 17% | 38% → 30% as the pool grew |
| Drops that are fragments/noise | 67% | **20%**, then 0 in the final run |
| Why the Judge drops | "pure noise", "nonsensical" | "too broad and highly competitive", "standard commodity with no underserved niche" |
| Non-English candidates | 2 per night | 0 (6 documents skipped at the gate) |

The last row is the qualitative change worth naming: the pipeline stopped failing at *syntax* and
started disagreeing about *substance*, which is what a working funnel looks like.

## Two more bugs this phase found, both in existing code

**1. A replay broke after a live night.** `gate.sh`'s L0 replay probe started failing with
`fixture 'arctic_shift' has no response for ...?after=2026-09-20&limit=5&sort=desc&subreddit=...`
— because the live runs advanced the watermark, and the fixture holds the `after=` value from the
day it was recorded. The replay was depending on *state a live run had changed*, which is the
opposite of what a replay is for. Fixed in `net.py`: fixture lookup retries with cursor parameters
(`after`, `before`, `since`, `page`, `offset`, `cursor`, …) removed, everything else still matching
exactly — so a request for one subreddit can never be served another subreddit's recording. When two
recordings differ only by cursor, the newest cursor wins, deterministically.

**2. Thirteen eval cases were mislabelled, and the reason was the P7 bug itself.** The P5 cases were
seeded from the pre-P7 candidate pool with `expected_keep` from the documented median split. Once the
Judge had seen those candidates live, it dropped 13 of them ("conversational noise", "fragmented
text") — the *labels* said keep because the phrase-quality score was high, which is precisely the
mislabelling the Judge gate exists to catch.

The seed script always said the median split was provisional ("the P3 Judge replaces this
judgement"); now it does. `seed_evals.py` labels a case from the **Judge's verdict where one exists**
(recording the verdict's date, model, confidence and reason in the case's notes) and from the
threshold where it does not, saying which. Re-seeded:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Cases with a verified keep/drop | 2 | **18** |
| Cases labelled by the Judge | 0 | 18 (each with its reason in the notes) |
| Cases labelled by the threshold | 50 | 32 (notes say no verdict exists yet) |
| keep / drop split | 27 / 23 | **15 / 35** |
| Failing cases | 13 | **0** |

The split moving toward drops is not a regression: it is a set that can now catch the failure mode
that actually occurs in this system (keeping rubbish), rather than one that mostly agrees with the
scorer it was derived from.

## The honest gaps this phase leaves

1. **The lake is still the binding constraint.** 1,181 minable texts → 79 candidates → 10 kept, and
   only 5 of the 10 come from more than one document. Velocity percentiles over a pool where most
   phrases appear once are *arithmetic on noise*. **Twelve registered Tier-S sources still have no
   plugin** (GDELT, Bluesky, Steam, iTunes, Mastodon, OpenAlex, WordPress, Shopify, itch.io, Google
   Books, Common Crawl, suggest-autocomplete). That work is worth more than any further matcher
   refinement, and this phase's numbers say so.
2. **The Judge is charitable to clean-but-empty phrases.** It kept "excellent filament" (a person
   praising their filament) at confidence 0.70, and reframed the fragment "spaghetti filament" into
   an opportunity. Structure is solved; semantic emptiness is not.
3. **Single-document candidates are kept.** A ≥2-document gate was planned and rejected *by
   measurement*: "chisel storage" came from exactly one post. The count is reported and used to rank
   shared phrases above lonely ones; it is not a gate.
4. **The taxonomy is still small** (159 core nouns, 61 form nouns, 10 categories). It now rejects for
   the right reasons, but coverage of the *lake's* vocabulary remains the next lever after volume.

## What this phase cost

$0. Every provider call was inside a free tier (Gemini free tier for judgement, Groq verified as the
working fallback). The whole investigation — two live collections, five mining runs, three live Judge
runs over ~70 candidates — cost 27k prompt + 17k completion tokens.

## Bugs this phase found in its own work

* **A comment inside a `.split()` word list became data.** The first P7 edit explained itself inside
  the stop-word string, so `"saw"`, `saw` and `saw,` became stop words and the saw products silently
  vanished from mining. `test_stop_words_are_words_and_never_a_product_noun` now fails on any
  non-alphabetic entry *and* on any word the taxonomy needs (`saw`, `can` — a verb and a product).
* **A stop word that is also a product noun deletes products.** `saw` and `can` are both; the guard
  test names the class.
* **An "improvement" that lost a find**: the planned ≥2-document gate would have deleted
  `chisel storage`. Measured before shipping, not after.
