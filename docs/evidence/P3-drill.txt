RUN 2 is not a regression: it is the free tier's limit, spent by the drill itself. Gemini
now answers HTTP 429 (quota exceeded) for any request, so the live call cannot succeed until
the quota resets. The gateway behaved correctly — it retried the transient 429 per entry,
walked the whole chain in order, and degraded with a reason rather than retrying blindly.

The finding worth keeping (docs/LESSONS.md §6.5): the spec's per-gate call budgets (Judge
~20 calls/night, Writer ~30) assume a paid tier. At ~700 tokens per Judge call, one night's
20 calls is ~14k tokens — more than a free tier allows in a day. Either the budgets shrink to
the free tier and the cache carries the load, or a paid key is required for nightly runs.
That decision is P4's, not P3's, and it is now written down where it will be needed.
