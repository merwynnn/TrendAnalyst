Trend Analyst · Product Blueprint

Find gaps. Validate ideas. Estimate revenue.

A market-intelligence engine in two stages: Discovery finds underserved product gaps, Validation scores your idea and projects revenue with a confidence range.

1 · The pipeline (4 layers)

Cheap deterministic code scans thousands of signals. The LLM only sees survivors. Tier-A quota is spent only on the top 5%.

L0 · CollectBroad, cheap, no-key sources onlyHN, Reddit archive, Bluesky, Mastodon, autocomplete, WordPress, Shopify, Steam, Wikipedia pageviews, OpenAlex… nightly cron, ~10 min.
↓
L1 · MineExtract phrases, measure velocity, prune 95%Complaint mining + EWMA z-scores per category. Keeps ~100–200 candidates/night. No paid calls here.
↓
L2 · EnrichDeep data on top-K onlyeBay sold/active, Best Buy trending, app stores, reviews. Token-bucket scheduler, 24h cache. This is where quotas are spent — sparingly.
↓
L3 · DecideScore, fad-check, briefGap score + fad flag + P10/P50/P90 revenue + 1-page brief. Indexed for prompt search.

3 · The gap score (simple version)

Five 0–100 sub-scores, weighted. High = better opportunity (except saturation, inverted).

Demand velocity (30%)Is interest growing, seasonally adjusted?
Low saturation (25%)Weak incumbents, fragmented market?
Buyer pain (20%)Bad ratings, complaints, returns?
Money (15%)Healthy price ($15–55), margin, repeat buys?
Feasibility (10%)Shippable by an indie? No hazmat / patents?
Fad flag (gate)Spike vs. sustained trend. Fads get flagged, not built.
MGS = 0.30·DV + 0.25·(100−SS) + 0.20·SP + 0.15·MP + 0.10·FE

Revenue is a range, never a point: P50 $18k/mo (P10 $7k – P90 $34k). If P10 doesn't cover your MOQ, skip — even at score 85.

Example: DV 82 + SS 30 + SP 70 + MP 60 + FE 90 → MGS ≈ 72. High demand, weak incumbents, real complaints — worth enriching.

4 · Where the LLM sits

Three gates. ~50 calls/night. Fits free tiers (Gemini + Groq + Cerebras with fallback).

GateJobCost
Planner (weekly)Turns "notion assets <$30" into a search plan: keywords, sources, budgets~1 call
Judge (nightly)Keep / drop + fad probability + what to enrich, with cited quotes~20 calls
Writer (nightly)1-page brief: verdict, players, risks, angles, revenue reasoning~30 calls

LLM never scores numbers or scrapes. No quotes + URLs → auto-reject. Identical inputs are cached for 30 days.
